Design system & navigation

The building blocks shared by every page. Styles live in css/base.css (tokens & components) and css/navbar.css (the header above). Resize the window below 1024px to see the mobile menu.

Navigation

  • Desktop (≥ 1024px): logo left, five links centred, search / wishlist / bag on the right. The current page is marked with aria-current="page" and a permanent underline.
  • Mobile & tablet: hamburger opens an off-canvas panel with a focus trap, Escape-to-close, scrim click-to-close and scroll lock. The page behind becomes inert.
  • Search: the magnifier (or the / key) opens a live product search. Arrow keys move through results.
  • Bag & wishlist: drawers built on native <dialog>; counts update instantly across pages and tabs.
