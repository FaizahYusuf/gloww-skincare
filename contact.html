FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I place an order online?

Yes. Add products to your bag and choose Secure checkout. You'll enter your shipping details and pay on Stripe's secure checkout page, then return here to see your order confirmation and reference.

Which products suit sensitive-feeling skin?

Our Apricot Comfort Day Cream and Gentle Foaming Cleanser were designed with comfort in mind. Every skin is different, so we always recommend a patch test before first use — and speaking with a professional about any specific skin concern.

How do I build a routine?

Start simple: cleanse, treat, moisturize. Our morning and evening rituals show the order we recommend, and the Journal explains each step in more detail.

Where are my bag and wishlist saved?

They're stored in this browser, on this device only. Customer accounts aren't available yet, so your selection won't appear on other devices, and clearing your browser data will remove it.

Are the descriptions medical advice?

No. Our products are cosmetics designed to care for the look and feel of skin, and our descriptions are written in cosmetic terms. For any skin condition, please consult a qualified professional.
